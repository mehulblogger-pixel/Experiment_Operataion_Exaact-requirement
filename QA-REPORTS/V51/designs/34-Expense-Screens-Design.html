The new expense screens

You said the voucher page is confusing, "What for" cannot be typed, items are not shown on screen, and approvers can edit claims. These pictures show how it will look and work instead. The figures are an example for Test Inspector in November 2026.

What stays exactly as today
  • One statement per person per month, the approval chain you checked tonight (N+1 → Director above ₹20,000 → Finance), Finance marks Paid, and the printed statement.
  • Every voucher already on opstest keeps its figures and history. Nothing is deleted.
  • Travel by own vehicle is still worked out as km × your rate (Bike ₹6/km, Car ₹12/km today).
What changes
  • No grid with 12 columns. You add one expense at a time, in two short steps, and see every expense as a clear line with its amount.
  • Every expense is for one of your jobs, or for an "other expense" type from the list your admin keeps (your rule V1).
  • Approvers see the same clear list and press Approve or Send back. They never edit your claim (your rule V2).
  • The working-days part (hours per day) is filled from your jobs automatically and moves to its own tab, out of the way.
