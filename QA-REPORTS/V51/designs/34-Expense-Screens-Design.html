The new expense screens

You said: "What the inspector works for, he is getting paid for. It is simple and short. Suggest something wherein no wrong can be done by the inspector." This version is built on exactly that. The amounts in the pictures are examples; you set the real ones.

The inspector does not fill in a claim. He confirms the expenses of each day he worked, when he closes that day. The program fills in the job, client, site, date, distance, rate and allowance. The inspector only adds a bill (with its photo) when there is one.

How it flows

Coordinator allocates the job→ Inspector inspects→ Inspector closes the day and confirms its expenses→ They land in his claim for the period by themselves→ At period end: one tap, Submit→ N+1 → Director (above your amount) → Finance→ Paid

This also ends a problem found in the audit: today a cost entered when closing a job and the same cost on the monthly voucher are counted twice in profit. In the new design they are one record.

What the inspector cannot do wrong

Mistake or misuseHow the design stops it
Claiming a day he did not workExpenses exist only on a day he closed on his own allocated job, after checking in. No job, no expense.
Wrong job number, client or siteNever typed. Taken from the job.
Inflating the distanceThe usual distance for each site is remembered once the first claim to it is approved. A different figure needs a reason and is shown to the approver in amber.
Wrong rate or a higher allowanceNever typed. Rate per km and daily allowance come from his grade in settings.
A bill without proof, or over the limitPhoto required to add it. Above the grade limit it is flagged to the approver (or refused, if you prefer).
The same bill twiceOne allowance per day per job; a bill with the same date, kind and amount is refused as a repeat.
Remembering expenses weeks laterConfirmed when closing the day; after your deadline (e.g. 3 days) the day is locked and only his N+1 can reopen it.
Counting a cost twice in profitOne record: the day's expenses are the claim's lines.
Approver changing the claimApprove or send back only (your rule V2).

Your point 4 — "other expenses"

My recommendation: none, for inspectors. If he is paid for the work he does, every expense belongs to a job day.

  • A mobile bill becomes a fixed monthly allowance on the person's record (e.g. ₹500 ex.), added to the claim automatically. He claims nothing.
  • Office staff (coordinators, managers) who travel for the office, not for a job, can keep a short "not for a job" list, switched on only for them. Off by default.
