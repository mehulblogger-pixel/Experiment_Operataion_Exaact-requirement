# Simplify first: one page (18 Nov 2026)

**Where we are today:** 438 screens · 330 company settings · 19 roles · 16 items in the left menu · 7 separate places to set "who approves" · 114 tiles in the area pages. That is the size of an ERP. A TPIA company of 20–50 people needs about a quarter of it to start.

**The rule from now on:** each module week is **test + cut**. Something is hidden, merged or removed every week, and nothing new is added unless real use shows the need. *Hiding never deletes data:* a switched-off module keeps all its records and comes back with one switch (Admin → Modules & features, which already exists).
