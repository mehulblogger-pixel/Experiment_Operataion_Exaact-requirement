The four safety rules
Rule 1

Never edit or delete a record you did not create. That includes every demo record already on the system.

Rule 2

Label everything you create as a test. Put UAT-2026- in front of any reference, and UAT TEST - in front of any person's or company's name.

Rule 3

Do not issue real financial or legal documents. Draft them, read them, then stop. See the do-not-press list below.

Rule 4

A technical error is always a defect. If you see SQLSTATE, PDOException, Fatal error, or a wall of file names, stop, screenshot it, and mark the step FAIL. A plain-English message is the system working correctly — that is different.

Rule 3

Do not press these

Drafting and previewing is safe. These create real documents or assert that real money moved.

  • InvoicesFinalise, issue or send an invoice — a real financial document, possibly numbered in a legal sequence, possibly e-mailed to the client.
  • VouchersMark PAID — asserts money has actually left the business.
  • CandidateStandard offer letter, appointment letter, confirmation of employment, internship offer, relieving & experience letter — real employment documents.
  • ReportIssue or finalise a report on a real client's work — a client may rely on it.
  • Careers pagePublishing a live job advert — real people may apply.
  • AnywhereAnything that e-mails or messages an outside party.

The line is: does anyone outside this UAT team receive it, rely on it, or does it assert money moved? If in doubt, do not press it — ask.
