# Pricing research — suggested starting numbers (22 Oct 2026)

The owner asked (22 Oct 2026): *"research this number and let me know or keep it open to
configure in admin panel, don't hardcode it."*

The app does both:

- **No prices are built into the app.** Every price, allowance and tax setting is saved by the
  platform owner. On **Plans, pricing & usage** these are module prices, the login price, the
  yearly discount, GST, the AI top-up and seat costs. Under **Companies** they are storage and
  the free trial.
- **A price that is not set means not for sale.** Customers see "Not for sale yet" and nothing is
  charged at a made-up figure.
- **Each box shows the suggested value below and the reason.** "Fill in the suggested values" puts
  them into the empty boxes only. Nothing is saved until you press **Save**.

## Read first

- **How the research was done.** All figures come from web searches on 22 Oct 2026. Most are from
  third-party listings (G2, Capterra, partners, blogs), which often disagree, and the vendors' own
  pricing pages could not be opened. Check the competitor prices on each vendor's own site before
  quoting them to customers.
- **EXAACT is priced differently from most competitors.** A company pays each **module once per
  company**, plus each **login (person)**. Most competitors charge per user instead, so their
  figures are translated in the table below.
- **Labels in the table:** **E** means supported directly by evidence; **J** means judgement.

## GST and payments

- **Place of supply.** When the buyer is GST-registered, the place of supply is the buyer's state.
  - Buyer in the same state as you: CGST + SGST.
  - Buyer in another state: IGST.
  - Buyer without a GSTIN: taxed at your own state's rate.
- **Tax invoice (Rule 46).** It must show your GSTIN, a consecutive number per financial year of at
  most 16 characters, the buyer's GSTIN, the place of supply, the SAC code, the taxable value, the
  rate and tax amounts, and whether reverse charge applies. The app issues one for every payment
  that carries GST, numbered like `EX/2026-27/0001`.
- **E-invoicing (IRN).** Compulsory only once yearly turnover passes ₹5 crore.
- **Razorpay fees.** About 2% plus 18% GST on that fee, so about 2.36% in total. Subscription card
  payments cost an extra 0.99%. Build this into your prices or your margin.
