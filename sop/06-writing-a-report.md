# Writing an inspection report — a step-by-step guide for the inspector

> This is for **you, the inspector** — the person who does the inspection and writes
> the report on a phone, tablet or laptop. **You do not need any computer knowledge.**
> Just do the steps in order. The app always shows you the **one next thing to do**;
> do that, and repeat until the report is issued.
>
> Keep this page open the first few times. After that you will not need it.

---

## The golden rule

At the top of every report there is a card called **"How to get this report issued."**
It is a checklist. **One step is always highlighted in blue and says `DO THIS NOW`.**

- **Blue step (`DO THIS NOW`)** → this is your next job. Do it.
- **Green tick (`DONE`)** → already finished. Skip it.
- **Red step (`BLOCKED`)** → something is missing; the card tells you what. Fix that, and it turns blue.

**You never have to guess.** If you are ever lost, look at the blue step and do what it says.

---

## Do this once (before your first report): set your signature

Your signature is put on every report you sign — **automatically**. You set it **one time**.

1. Open the menu → **Reporting** → open any report → click **✍ My signature** (top right).
   *(Or just click **✍ My signature** whenever the report card asks you to.)*
2. **Draw your signature** with your finger (on a phone) or mouse — or tap **Upload** and choose a photo of your signature.
3. Tap **Save signature.**

Done. You never do this again. From now on your signature appears on every report you prepare or approve, on its own.

> If a report ever says **"Inspector signature not on file"**, it means this step was skipped. Do it now and the report will be signed.

## The 6 steps to a finished report

Below is the whole journey. Each step matches a line on the blue checklist card.

### Step 1 — Open your report

1. Menu → **Reporting** → **Report register** (this is the list of all reports).
2. Find your report and tap it. *(Coming from your job? There is an **Open report** button on the job.)*
3. You see the card **"How to get this report issued."** Look at the blue step.

If a report does not exist yet, your coordinator creates it, or use **+ New report**, pick the report type (for example **Inspection Report** or **Fire Extinguisher Inspection Report**), and save.

### Step 2 — Read the plan, and let the app fill the scope for you

Most of the top of the report (client, vendor, PO, dates) is **already filled in** from the job. You do not type it.

If a **QAP** (the quality plan / ITP) has been uploaded for the job, you will see a **📋 QAP** box:

1. Tap the QAP file name to **read the plan** while you work.
2. Tap **✨ Auto-fill from QAP.** The app reads the plan and fills in:
   - the **inspection scope** table (clause, activity, quantum, inspection type), and
   - the **items** table (from the linked purchase order — quantities and descriptions).
3. A green message tells you how many rows were added. **You only add the observations.**

> No QAP uploaded? Ask your coordinator to upload it on the job — then this button appears.
> Nothing happened? The file may be a scanned photo with no readable text; type the scope, or upload a clearer QAP.

### Step 3 — Fill the boxes — **type or just speak**

Now fill the inspection details and your findings (Observations, Conclusion, Remarks).

**You can speak instead of typing** — in your own language:

1. At the top of the fill screen, set **Dictation → Language** to your language
   (English, हिन्दी, ગુજરાતી, मराठी, தமிழ், తెలుగు, বাংলা, ಕನ್ನಡ, മലയാളം, ਪੰਜਾਬੀ).
2. On any big text box, tap **🎤 Dictate** and **speak**. Your words appear as you talk.
   Tap **⏹ Stop** when done.
3. If you spoke roughly, or in your own language, tap **✨ Polish / translate.**
   The app rewrites it into clean, correct report English — **without changing any of your facts, numbers or findings.**

> Example: say (in Hindi) *"body theek hai, koi dent nahi, gauge green zone mein hai, weight sahi hai"* → tap **✨ Polish / translate** → it becomes
> *"Body condition satisfactory with no dents; pressure gauge reading within the green/operable range; charge weight within tolerance."*

You can still type by hand if you prefer. **✒️ Improve wording** expands short notes like "dimension ok" into a proper sentence.

> The **🎤 Dictate** and **✨ Polish / translate** buttons need the internet and work best in the **Chrome** browser. If you don't see 🎤, use Chrome; if you don't see ✨, your company has not switched on the AI helper (typing still works).

### Step 4 — Add photos

On the **Photographs** box:

1. Tap **📷 Take photo** (camera) or **⬆ Upload photo(s)**.
2. Pick or take your pictures. They are shrunk automatically and tagged with the time and place.

### Step 5 — Submit for approval

When the body is filled, the blue step becomes **Submit for review.**

1. First, look at the **Inspection completeness check** panel. Every item must be a green ✓.
   - A red ✗ means something is missing. Each red item has a **Fix →** link — tap it and it takes you to exactly where to fix it (for example **✗ No inspector signature → Add my signature**).
2. When all checks pass, tap **✓ Submit for review** (on the card, or in the completeness panel).
3. The report goes to your **approver**. You are done for now.

> It will not let you submit while checks are failing — that is on purpose, so a half-finished report never goes out. Fix the red items first.

### Step 6 — After approval: the report is issued

- Your approver gets the report in their **"Awaiting my approval"** list. They tap **✓ Approve**, or **↩ Send back** with a comment.
- **If it comes back to you:** it will be marked **Sent back**, with the reason. Fix it and submit again (Step 5).
- **Once approved and issued:** the card shows **✓ Issued.** Your signature is stamped on automatically. Tap **📄 Download PDF** (or **📄 Download report** for the Word/company format) to get the final file.

That's the whole report. 🎉

## What the words on the card mean

| You see | It means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Blue step · **DO THIS NOW** | Your next job | Do exactly what the step says |
| **DONE** (green tick) | Already finished | Nothing — move on |
| **BLOCKED** (red) | Something is missing | The step tells you what; fix it |
| **Your approval is needed** | *(Approvers only)* a report is waiting on you | Tap Approve / Send back / Reject |
| **✓ Issued** | Finished and locked | Download the PDF |

---

## If something goes wrong

| It says / happens | Why | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **"8 completeness check(s) still failing"** | Some required boxes are empty | Open the **Inspection completeness check** panel, find the red ✗ items, tap each **Fix →** |
| **"No inspector signature"** | Your signature isn't saved | Tap **Add my signature** → draw or upload → Save (see *Do this once*) |
| **"No approver could be resolved"** | Nobody is set to approve this report | Ask your coordinator to set your approver (Inspection Reports → Approver mapping), or pick the approver on the report, then submit again |
| **✨ Auto-fill from QAP did nothing** | The QAP is a scanned image with no readable text | Type the scope by hand, or ask for a text-based QAP |
| **I don't see the 🎤 Dictate button** | The browser doesn't support voice | Open the app in **Chrome** |
| **I don't see ✨ Polish / translate** | The AI helper isn't switched on for your company | Typing still works; ask your admin to enable it under Settings → AI |
| **The report is locked / greyed out** | It is already **Issued** (finished) | You can't change an issued report. If it must change, ask a manager to **Reissue as a new revision** |
| **I picked the wrong report type** | — | Ask your coordinator; an issued report keeps its number, a draft can be replaced |

## One-page checklist (print or keep on your phone)

**Once, ever:** ✍ Set My signature.

**Every report:**
- [ ] 1. Open the report → read the blue step
- [ ] 2. Read the **QAP** → tap **✨ Auto-fill from QAP** (fills scope + items)
- [ ] 3. Fill the findings — **🎤 Dictate** in your language → **✨ Polish / translate**
- [ ] 4. Add **📷 photos**
- [ ] 5. Clear every red ✗ (tap **Fix →**) → **✓ Submit for review**
- [ ] 6. After approval → **📄 Download PDF**

**Remember:** do the **blue `DO THIS NOW`** step. That is always your next job.

---

## Frequently asked

**Do I have to type in perfect English?**
No. Speak in your own language, tap **✨ Polish / translate**, and it becomes proper report English. Your facts and numbers are never changed.

**Do I sign every report?**
No. You set your signature **once**. After that it is added to every report automatically.

**Can I lose my work if the internet drops?**
Save often. What you have typed stays in the boxes; press the button again if a save is refused because the session timed out.

**Who sees the report before the client?**
Your approver. Nothing goes out until it is approved and issued.

**Where is the finished report?**
On the report, tap **📄 Download PDF** (plain) or **📄 Download report** (your company / client format). It is also verifiable by the QR code printed on it.
